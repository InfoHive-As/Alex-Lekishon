Project Components & Strategic Pillars

Component 1: Integrated HWC Management Establishment of 1 central EarthRanger command center in Rumuruti (Laikipia) and 2 subcommand centers in Meru & Nanyuki; LoRaWAN telemetry expansion and digital radio networks.
Component 2: Zoonotic Disease Surveillance Syndromic surveillance protocols, laboratory testing equipment, and capacity training for rangers at the human-wildlife-livestock interface.
Component 3: Community Conservation & Livelihoods Capitalizing the Kenya Conservancies Fund to disburse small grants across 15 community conservancies, supporting predator-proof bomas and water pans.
Component 4 & 5: Knowledge Management & M&E Institutionalizing robust Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E) systems, Project Implementation Reports (PIRs), and national gender-inclusive communication strategies.
