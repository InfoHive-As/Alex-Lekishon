Community Needs Assessment: Final Evaluation Report

Grassroots Development Priorities, Education Inclusivity & Women Empowerment in Rural Kenya

📄 Oliveseed_Community_Needs_Assessment.pdf · Lead Consultant: Alex Lekishon, MA

1. Purpose & Scope

This comprehensive evaluation was commissioned by Oliveseed Foundation (USA/Kenya) to establish baseline socio-economic benchmarks, identify community-identified vulnerabilities, and design an actionable multi-year intervention roadmap for rural communities.

2. Key Research Findings

Youth Education Access Identified critical textbook shortages and lack of digital literacy tools across 8 primary schools.
Women's Economic Groups Documented high potential for indigenous craft cooperatives and sustainable beekeeping enterprises.
Clean Water & Sanitation 42% of community households travel over 4km daily for domestic water points during dry seasons.
Community Governance Recommended formal capacity building workshops for village elders and women leadership committees.

3. Methodological Framework

Conducted participatory rural appraisal (PRA), 24 key informant interviews (KII), and 6 focus group discussions (FGDs) with pastoralist women and youth leaders. All instruments and reports were authored and synthesized by Alex Lekishon.
