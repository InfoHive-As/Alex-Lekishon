Executive Summary

The 2024 Annual Report provides an in-depth review of the Laikipia Conservancies Association's programmatic achievements, financial sustainability, and landscape governance across more than 2.5 million acres in northern Kenya. Despite regional climate pressures, LCA and its member conservancies demonstrated historic resilience in rangeland management, human-wildlife conflict mitigation, and community livelihood enhancement.

Strategic Pillars & Outcomes

  • 1. Landscape Rangeland Restoration Rehabilitated over 45,000 hectares of degraded grasslands through planned holistic rotational grazing.
  • 2. Strategic Grants & Mobilization Secured critical multi-year funding streams with GEF-8 ($3M) and GBFF ($2M) in collaboration with global conservation partners.
  • 3. MEL & Indicator Dashboards Institutionalized unified monitoring protocols across member conservancies, establishing real-time wildlife tracking.
  • 4. Communications & Advocacy Produced 20+ newsletters, donor briefs, and national policy engagements under communications leadership of Alex Lekishon.
Communications & Grants Leadership Highlights (Alex Lekishon)

"Under the strategic stewardship of Alex Lekishon, LCA completely redesigned its digital presence, launched a high-engagement monthly newsletter series, and packaged key technical project outcomes into compelling donor narratives that were instrumental in securing major bilateral funding awards." — LCA Board of Trustees

Looking Ahead to 2025 – 2026

As we transition into the next funding cycle, LCA remains focused on scaling community conservancy enterprise models, expanding ecological corridors connecting Laikipia and Samburu, and institutionalizing climate-resilient rangeland policies at both county and national levels.
