Cover Story · Major Funding

Unlocking $5M in Biodiversity Funding for Northern Kenya Rangelands

In a landmark victory for community-led conservation, the Laikipia Conservancies Association contributed to the finalization of grant proposals under GEF-8 ($3 Million) and the Global Biodiversity Framework Fund ($2 Million). Coordinated by our grants and communications team, these funds will directly empower local pastoralist conservancies to restore vital grazing lands and establish early-warning drought mechanisms.

Ranger Spotlight

Frontline Protectors: Female Rangers Lead Wildlife Census in Il Ngwesi

Over 60 community rangers completed advanced wildlife tracking and digital GPS monitoring, recording a notable recovery in Grevy's zebra populations across the central corridor.

"Effective conservation reporting bridges the gap between raw field telemetry and international donor partners who want to see transparent, community-owned results." — Alex Lekishon, Communications Officer
Digital Transformation

New LCA Web Platform Drives 60% Surge in Stakeholder Engagement

With modern information architecture, interactive donor impact maps, and an automated newsletter subscription engine, the overhauled LCA digital ecosystem has connected 32 member conservancies with over 15,000 monthly visitors across Kenya, Europe, and North America.
