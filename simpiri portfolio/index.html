About

I am a Communications, Public Relations, and Fundraising Specialist with over 5 years of experience in conservation, environmental management, and community development across Kenya. I specialize in designing and executing strategic communication frameworks that elevate institutional visibility, deepen donor engagement, and drive evidence-based decision-making.

My expertise spans grant writing and resource mobilization (contributing to successful proposals for USAID, GEF-8, GBFF, TNC, and Wyss Academy), Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL), digital media strategy, policy formulation, and executive stakeholder management. Trained in climate change resilience, environmental reporting, and digital marketing, I am committed to delivering innovative communication strategies that yield measurable outcomes.
